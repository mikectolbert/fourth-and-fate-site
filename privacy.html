Fourth & Fate — Privacy Policy

Developer: AfterPrime · Effective September 28, 2026

In short: Fourth & Fate does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The game has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it saves stays on your device.

What the game stores

To let you continue a run and keep your settings, the game saves these files in its private storage on your device:

  • your current run and any match in progress,
  • your unlocks and lifetime statistics,
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, text size, motion and display options),
  • a local diagnostic log, which you can view in the game and which never leaves the device.

None of these contain your name, contact details, location, contacts, photos, or any identifier for you or your device. They are not backed up to the cloud by the game. Uninstalling the game deletes all of them.

What the game does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request network access.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • It does not use the advertising ID.
  • It does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or any other personal data.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, chat or user-generated content.

The only device permission the game uses is vibration, for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings.

Purchases

Fourth & Fate is bought through Google Play. Google processes that purchase under Google's Privacy Policy; the developer receives no payment details. There are no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
